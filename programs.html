Class Attire

What to Wear to Class

Simple, consistent attire keeps class focused on dancing. Here's what each class needs.

Hip Hop

  • Sneakers
  • Black tights or leggings
  • JJDC T-shirt (or a plain fitted T-shirt until purchased)

Freestyle, Jazz & Contemporary

  • Black leotard
  • Black tights
  • Barefoot, foot undies or jazz shoes as instructed

Official JJDC Apparel

  • T-shirts, hoodies, jackets, tights, shorts and accessories
  • Required for examinations, competitions, workshops, performances and travelling teams
