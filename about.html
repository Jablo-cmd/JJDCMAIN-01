Our Story

A Studio Built Around Every Dancer

Jo Jackson Dance Company is a professional dance studio based at 8 Smartt Road, Nahoon, East London — built around a simple belief: every dancer, at every age and every level, deserves quality training in an environment where they feel encouraged rather than pressured.

The studio brings respected choreographers and industry professionals to East London, giving local dancers access to high-quality training without having to travel. Alongside that professional standard sits something just as important: a genuinely warm, family-oriented culture where students are supported at their own pace.

From a child's first tentative steps in a Pre Badge class to a competition dancer preparing for the stage, the studio's approach stays the same — strong technical foundations, real encouragement, and room for every dancer to grow into their own potential.

Meet Jo

The Heart Behind Jo Jackson Dance Company

Full profile now live

Jo Jackson leads the studio with a clear philosophy: technique matters, but so does how a dancer feels walking through the door. Training is thorough and consistent, because strong technique creates successful dancers — but growth always happens at each student's own pace.

That balance shows up in every decision at JJDC. Examinations are encouraged, never compulsory. Competitions are treated as opportunities to build resilience and confidence, not sources of pressure. Above all, every dancer matters — not just the ones aiming for the podium.

Teaching Philosophy

Dance Education Is About More Than Movement

At Jo Jackson Dance Company, dance education develops character, confidence and lifelong skills that carry far beyond the studio.

  • Strong technique creates successful dancers, so training is thorough and consistent.
  • Every student develops at their own pace — growth is never rushed.
  • Versatility is encouraged across styles, not narrow specialisation.
  • Examinations are encouraged, never compulsory.
  • Competitions are treated as opportunities for growth, not pressure.
  • Every dancer matters, regardless of ambition or ability.

Student Growth Journey

Growth That Lasts Beyond the Studio

Every dancer's path looks different, but the journey follows the same story of steady, supported growth.

  1. First Lesson

    A warm welcome into the studio, whatever a dancer's age or experience.

  2. Confidence

    Finding comfort in the room, the routines and the community.

  3. Technique

    Consistent training builds strong, versatile technical foundations.

  4. Performance

    Taking technique to the stage through showcases and studio performances.

  5. Competition

    For those who want it, competition teams build resilience and teamwork.

  6. Achievement

    Milestones reached at each dancer's own pace, celebrated along the way.

  7. Lifelong Memories

    Friendships, confidence and discipline that carry on far beyond the studio.

Studio Culture

A Place That Feels Like Family

Step into JJDC and the first thing you'll notice isn't the choreography — it's the culture. Dancers cheer each other on. Older students mentor younger ones. Instructors know every student by name.

That culture doesn't happen by accident. It's built through a genuinely positive, encouraging environment where dancers push each other to improve, rather than compete against one another.
