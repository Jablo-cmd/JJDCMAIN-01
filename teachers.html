JJDC Teaching Philosophy

Our teachers are passionate professionals dedicated to developing confident, versatile dancers. Each brings a unique perspective, advanced technical training, and a commitment to creating an inclusive, supportive learning environment where every student can grow.

Featured Teacher

Kirt Ruiters

Dancer · Choreographer · Teacher

Kirt Ruiters' journey with Jo Jackson Dance Co. began at the age of 8, when he first started training at JJDC. From these early foundations, dance has taken Kirt across South Africa and onto the international stage.

Kirt has travelled and competed throughout South Africa in Freestyle and Hip Hop, achieving Champion status. At just 13 years old, he competed at the Hip Hop World Championships, and he has also been awarded Provincial Colours for Dance.

His professional training continued at the Waterfront Theatre College in Cape Town, where he studied Dance, Drama, and Musical Theatre, completing advanced levels in Ballet, Modern, and Tap. Today, Kirt is a passionate and versatile dancer and choreographer committed to teaching and creating expressive, dynamic movement.

JJDC Teacher

Debbie Muller

Dancer · Teacher

Debbie Muller's passion for music and dance began in childhood and has continued to grow throughout her journey as both a dancer and teacher.

Her formal dance journey began in high school when dance classes were introduced at her school. Shortly afterwards, she discovered Jo Jackson Dance Studio in Stirling, where she completed her Hip Hop examination in 2004 and participated in competitions in 2007.

When Jo relocated, Debbie continued her dance training at Nicky Swales Dance Studio, where she completed her freestyle examinations, with her final examination taking place in 2012.

Debbie's passion for teaching developed soon afterwards when she began teaching for Nicky Swales at Grens Pre-School and Primary School. In 2016, she was also honoured to serve as a judge at the JJDC All Styles Dance Festival.

Following Nicky Swales' retirement, Debbie was delighted to reconnect with Jo Jackson Dance Studio when Jo returned to East London. She joined the JJDC team and has since been teaching at Gimmies Kleuterskool and Ooskus Gimnasium.

Alongside teaching, Debbie has continued to enjoy performing. She has appeared in productions including Elements and Beat It, and most recently performed in Night with the Stars, where her group's routine was awarded Best Performance in their category.

Today, Debbie's love for dance and teaching continues to flourish. She is passionate about inspiring young dancers, sharing her experience and being part of the supportive and creative community at Jo Jackson Dance Studio.

Her Philosophy

"Dance is more than learning steps; it is about discovering confidence, expressing yourself, and finding joy in the journey."

— Debbie Muller
