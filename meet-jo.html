MEET JO

Jo Jackson

Founder & Director

Jo Jackson is a passionate dance professional whose journey in performance began at six years old and developed into a career shaped by excellence, discipline and creativity.

She continues to lead Jo Jackson Dance Co and Promotions with warmth, vision and a deep commitment to helping dancers grow in confidence, artistry and performance.

Her Journey

From early classical training to a defining performing arts path

Jo's journey in the performing arts began at the age of six. She began training in Classical Dance and Modern Dance, and at age 12 discovered High Energy Freestyle through SADTA — a defining moment that shaped her early direction.

She subsequently built a career in dance and achieved numerous competition titles and qualifications through SADTA, IDTA and HFPA. Today she is licentiate qualified and respected as an adjudicator at championship competitions across South Africa.

Beyond the Dance Floor

A personal story of creativity, family and stage presence

The Art of Producing

Turning ideas into thoughtful, memorable performances

Jo has a strong passion for producing and directing shows. She enjoys taking a production from the first idea through to the final curtain call.

Her goal is to create productions that entertain, inspire and create lasting memories.

Her Philosophy

For Jo, dance is so much more than movement.

It is a language of passion, confidence, connection, inspiration and memories. Every step is an invitation to grow, every performance is an opportunity to bring people together.

Passion

Jo invests heart and energy into every rehearsal, performance and production.

Confidence

Her work is grounded in the belief that discipline builds both skill and self-belief.

Connection

She values the moments that bring dancers, families and audiences closer together.

Inspiration

Jo seeks to inspire every dancer to discover their own voice on stage.

Memories

Her productions are created to be remembered long after the final curtain falls.

The Journey Continues

Her journey continues to inspire those around her, both on and off the stage.
